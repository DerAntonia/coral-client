Client and launcher for Linux and Windows.

## New

- **Hitbox module:** one table for all kinds with a main colour, "Visible" and "Override colour" per kind, a new "Everything else" row, and a smoothed/raw choice (with its own colour) for players. The old smoothed/raw switches at the top are gone.
- **Mip sharpness:** slider next to Mipmapping (0 = soft, -1 = sharper).
- **Panorama** as menu background image.
- **Modified view model:** hand bobbing.
- **Server pause-menu dialogs** and dialog answer window.
- **Inventory:** double-click collect in crafting tables and other menus.
- **Display entity animations** glide like Vanilla.
- Module detail view is clipped at the panel edge; the hint lines at the bottom of menus are gone.

## Faster

- Less CPU for text layout, signs, chests, the closed chat and flat items on the ground; GPU skinning for dropped items and projectiles.
